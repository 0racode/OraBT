## ExamWiseNG questions
798 practice questions (Biology, Chemistry, Economics, English Language, Government, Mathematics, Physics) with
explanations, from https://github.com/aliuoluyemi7-prog/ExamWiseNG. The repository has no licence file. The author gave
permission to the PastQ CBT owner to use the questions for educational purposes only, not for commercial use. The
written confirmation is kept with the project records.
Keep PastQ CBT free of charge and of paid features or advertising while these questions are included, and remove them
if the author asks.

## MySchool past questions
Past questions and answers (JAMB/UTME, later WAEC and NECO) copied from https://myschool.ng/ with MySchool's written
permission for this free educational app. Conditions: the app stays free with no ads or fees, MySchool is credited with a
link to https://myschool.ng/, and the questions are removed on request. The collector (tools/collect_myschool.py) runs
slowly with an honest user agent. Questions with images are skipped for now. The written confirmation is kept with the
project records.
Changes made: added to the shared PastQ question library with type "utme" (jamb), "wassce" (waec) or "neco" and the exam
year; explanations are MySchool's own where they exist. Duplicates of questions already in the library were dropped.

## Nigerianscholar (Ulearngo)
Written permission (email from permissions@nigerianscholars.com) for bulk extraction of their full question banks and
answer keys, to be shared with the app developer and included in this free (no ads, no fees) app. Conditions: clear credit
to Nigerianscholar (Ulearngo) with a link to https://app.ulearngo.com, and the content is removed on request. Keep the
original email with the project records. Scope clarified by their representative (video call, also sent as text): only questions
from their web address may be used; content from their mobile apps on the app stores may NOT be used. Keep that text with the
project records. No Ulearngo questions have been imported yet.

Update (written confirmation from permissions@nigerianscholars.com, subject "Written Confirmation - Access to app.ulearngo.com
Content"): permitted to collect questions, answers and explanations from the Ulearngo web app at app.ulearngo.com, including
content that normally needs a sign-in, for this free (no ads, no fees) app, with credit and a link, removable on request.
Mobile-app content from the app stores remains out of scope. Collect slowly (their robots.txt asks for a 5 second delay).
